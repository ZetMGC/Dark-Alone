# Происхождение face.png

2026-10-28, встроенный image_gen.imagegen через навык imagegen, без CLI. PNG сохранён без редактирования. Вложения Mouthwashing служили ориентиром в обсуждении; сами скриншоты генератору не передавались и в ассет не вставлялись. В Godot импорт ограничен 256×256.

Полный запрос:

> Create an ORIGINAL game production texture, not a portrait illustration or model render. Single square FRONT ORTHOGRAPHIC HEAD TEXTURE for a low polygon realistic retro psychological horror game character. An ordinary tired adult male 35-45 with long narrow face, slight asymmetry, olive pale skin, subtle under-eye shadows, brown eyes, closed relaxed mouth, dark short messy side parted hair, light stubble. NOT any existing game character. Mouthwashing era retro painted/photo-collage skin aesthetic, muted realistic coloring, 256x256-looking pixel-level detail, flat diffuse ambient illumination NO directional highlights or cast shadows, no dramatic lighting. Exact composition for projection on mesh: head centered, full head including top hair and chin, hair top at y=5%, chin at y=94%, head sides about x=14% and 86%, ears extending to x=8% and92%. Eyes centered at x=35% and65%, y=44%; tip of nose y=62%; mouth center y=77%. Straight on gaze, NO perspective or rotation. Entire square filled with texture: flat muted skin beige background outside head, NO neck shoulders clothes or body, NO text, no grid, no frames, NO baked 3D pixelation effects or scanlines. Clear natural eyes and eyelids small proportional human eyes, well-defined nose and lips. This is intended to wrap on an actual sculpted 3D face, preserve natural anatomy.
